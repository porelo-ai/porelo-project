# ST295 — A person joins and leaves an organization from the window

**Priority:** 🟰 Medium · **State:** Backlog

**What a user gains.** A person who cannot write an organization's repository asks to join it from the window by filing their card as a proposal, an owner accepts it there, and a member leaves an organization from the window, withdrawing their own card and roles and forgetting it on this computer.

**The test.** On a test organization, a person without write access proposes their card from the window; an owner sees it on the person's row in People and accepts it; the person then works as a member; another member leaves from the window, and their card, roles and the organization on that computer are gone.
