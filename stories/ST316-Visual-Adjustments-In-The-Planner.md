# ST316 — Visual adjustments in the planner

**Priority:** 🟰 Medium · **State:** Idea

**What a user gains.** The planning board reads at a glance: every story card and issue tile shows its T-shirt size beside its priority, folding a state's box hides the stories and issues in it while the column keeps its width, and a single card can be folded down to its heading with number and priority.

**The test.** Every story card and issue tile shows its size (XS to XL) beside its priority; folding the box "Estimated" hides its tiles, keeps its header and count and the column's width, while the column's own chevron still narrows it; folding one story card leaves only its heading with number and priority, the cards beside it open.

**Issues:** none yet
