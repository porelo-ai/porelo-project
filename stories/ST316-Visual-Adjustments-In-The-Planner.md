# ST316 — Visual adjustments in the planner

**Priority:** 🟰 Medium · **State:** Idea

**What a user gains.** The planning board reads at a glance: every story card and issue tile shows its T-shirt size beside its priority, and folding a state's box hides the stories and issues in it while the column keeps its width.

**The test.** On the planning board: every story card and every issue tile, wide or compact, shows its size (XS to XL) as a badge beside the priority; fold the box "Estimated" in ESTIMATION and its tiles are hidden, its header with name and count stays, and the column keeps its width; narrow the column with the column's own chevron and the tiles turn compact as before.

**Issues:** none yet
