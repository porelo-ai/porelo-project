# ST380 — A forge and Matrix of one's own, installed and seen

**Priority:** 🔼 High · **State:** Accepted

**What a user gains.** A person installs and uninstalls a forge or Matrix from the window, each alone, alike on their computer or a host; uninstall refuses while work is not at central. Under each machine both have a page: what waits for central or review, what agents ask, a jump to forge or client. The machine's page only says how each stands; My forges becomes Central forges, listing only those.

**The test.** On a fresh Ubuntu computer with Porelo from apt, a person installs Matrix, then the forge. Work waits on the forge page until brought to central, and uninstall refuses meanwhile; a pull request opens in the browser, an agent's question shows on Matrix's page. The same works on a host; uninstalling leaves each as before. On Windows and macOS this computer offers neither.

**Issues:** none yet
