# ST381 — A forge and Matrix of one's own on Windows and macOS

**Priority:** 🔽 Low · **State:** Idea

**What a user gains.** A person on Windows, whose Porelo runs in WSL, or on macOS, whose Porelo runs in a lightweight VM, gets a forge and Matrix of their own as a Linux user does: installed, seen and uninstalled from the window, either inside WSL or the VM, or on a host when that serves better, whichever the analysis finds sound.

**The test.** On Windows with Porelo in WSL and on macOS with its VM, a person installs Matrix, then the forge, from the window; the pages under this computer show them as on Linux, the forge and the Matrix client open from the desktop, and uninstalling leaves the computer as before.

**Issues:** none yet
